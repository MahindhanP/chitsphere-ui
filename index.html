FAQ

Common questions

How do we get started?

Contact us. We verify your company, create your organisation with an organisation code, and email your owner an activation link. There is no self-service sign-up.

Can one person work in several organisations?

Yes. One sign-in can belong to several organisations, with a different role in each. After signing in you pick the organisation, or enter its code on the sign-in page.

Can staff be limited to their branch?

Yes. Each member can see all branches or only the branches you assign, and gets roles built from templates such as Branch Manager, Accountant or Agent.

What happens when we reach a plan limit?

You see usage for users, branches, customers, groups and employees under Plan & usage. Near the limit we warn you; at the limit new records are blocked until the plan changes.

Is the accounting double-entry?

Yes. Vouchers post balanced journal entries with approval levels, fiscal periods, party and chit group ledgers, and reports such as trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet.
