FAQ

Common questions

Who can register?

Chit fund companies registered under the Chit Funds Act, 1982 or a state chit funds act. We verify your registration certificate before your organisation goes live.

Do we get our own app in the Play Store and App Store?

Yes, on every plan. We build and publish a separate app under your company’s name and icon, and handle store submissions and updates for you. It is usually live within 3–7 days of approval.

Whose developer account is the app published under?

By default we publish it under ChitSphere’s developer accounts, with your company as the app name. On Enterprise, it can be published under your own Google Play and Apple accounts so the store shows your company as the developer.

Will members see other organisations?

No. Your app connects only to your organisation, so members see only your chits, branding and contacts.

Can we bring our existing groups and members?

Yes. Running groups, subscribers and past auction results can be imported from Excel during setup.

Ready to move your chit business online?

Registration takes about 10 minutes. Have your registration certificate and PAN ready.
